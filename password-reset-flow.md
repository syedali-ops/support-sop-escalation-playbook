# Password Reset & ID Verification (Sample)

## Purpose
Help customers regain access without exposing the account to fraud.

## Flow
1. Customer reports a login problem.
2. Confirm the issue (wrong password, locked account, no access to email).
3. **Verify identity** using at least two approved checks, such as:
   - Name and registered email
   - Recent order details
   - Document or ID that matches the account
4. **Match check:** details provided must match the account exactly.
5. **If verified:** send the reset link to the *registered* email only.
6. **If not verified or details mismatch:** do not proceed. Explain the next step
   and escalate if fraud is suspected.
7. Document the outcome.

## Red flags (escalate)
- Customer cannot answer basic account questions
- Request to send the reset to a *new* email or phone number
- Repeated reset attempts in a short time
- Pressure or urgency used to skip verification

## Rule
Never share account details until identity is confirmed.
