# Support SOP & Escalation Playbook

Sample standard operating procedures, an escalation matrix, and case-note
templates for customer support and verification teams.

> All examples are fictional and created for demonstration. They contain no
> real company procedures or customer data.

## Contents
- [Refund SOP](refund-sop.md)
- [Password Reset & ID Verification](password-reset-flow.md)
- [Escalation Matrix](escalation-matrix.md)
- [Case Note Examples](case-note-examples.md)

## Why I built this
Clear SOPs, accurate notes and correct escalations are what keep support quality
consistent. These templates reflect practices I used in high-volume support work:
verify first, document everything, escalate with full context.
