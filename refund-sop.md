# Refund SOP (Sample)

## Purpose
Process refund requests accurately, consistently and with a full audit trail.

## Before you start
- Confirm you are speaking with the account holder (see ID verification).
- Open the order and review past contacts for earlier refund requests.

## Steps
1. **Verify the customer** using the approved identity checks.
2. **Locate the order** and confirm the item, date and amount.
3. **Check eligibility:**
   - Within the refund window?
   - Item eligible (not excluded by policy)?
   - No prior refund already issued for this order?
4. **Decide:**
   - Eligible → process the refund to the original payment method.
   - Not eligible → explain the reason clearly and offer alternatives.
   - Unclear or above your limit → escalate (see escalation matrix).
5. **Tell the customer** the amount, method and expected timeline.
6. **Document the case** using the case note template.

## Do not
- Refund to a different payment method without approval.
- Promise timelines you cannot guarantee.
- Skip documentation, even for small amounts.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Identity verified
- [ ] Eligibility checked
- [ ] Correct amount and method
- [ ] Customer informed
- [ ] Case note complete
