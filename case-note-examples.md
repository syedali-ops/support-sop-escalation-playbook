# Case Note Examples (Sample)

## Template
- **Issue:**
- **Verification done:**
- **Action taken:**
- **Outcome / next step:**
- **Escalated to (if any):**

## Weak note
> Customer called about refund. Done.

## Strong note
> **Issue:** Customer requested a refund for order #1234, delivered damaged.
> **Verification:** Name, registered email and order date matched.
> **Action:** Confirmed the item was within the refund window. Processed a full
> refund of ₹1,200 to the original payment method.
> **Outcome:** Customer informed the refund will arrive in 5-7 business days.
> **Escalated:** No.

## Escalation note example
> **Issue:** Customer cannot access the account and wants the reset sent to a new email.
> **Verification:** Could not confirm account details. Mismatch on registered email.
> **Action:** Did not send the reset. Explained the process to the customer.
> **Escalated to:** Fraud / Risk team, for review of a possible takeover attempt.
> **Next step:** Team will contact the registered email within 24 hours.

## Tips
- Write so a colleague can understand it in 10 seconds.
- Facts only, with no opinions or blame.
- Record what was done, not just what was said.
