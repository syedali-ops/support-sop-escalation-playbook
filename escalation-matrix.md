# Escalation Matrix (Sample)

| Situation | Escalate to | When | Include |
|---|---|---|---|
| Refund above agent limit | Team lead | Immediately | Order ID, amount, reason |
| Suspected fraud or ID mismatch | Fraud / Risk team | Immediately | Verification attempts, red flags |
| Repeated product or service issue | Quality / Operations lead | After 3+ similar cases | Case IDs, pattern summary |
| Customer requests a manager | Team lead | After one attempt to resolve | Summary of the issue and what you tried |
| Technical or system error | Technical support | When it blocks resolution | Screenshots, error text, steps taken |
| Legal threat or data request | Compliance / Legal | Immediately | Exact customer wording |

## Escalation rules
- Always add a clear summary so the next team doesn't need to ask again.
- Tell the customer what happens next and when to expect a reply.
- Follow up on open escalations within one business day.
